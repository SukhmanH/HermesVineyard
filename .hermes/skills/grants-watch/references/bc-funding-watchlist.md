# BC vineyard funding — the watchlist

What `grants-watch` monitors. Verified against program pages 2026-08-23; **statuses change with
intake windows, so every check re-reads these pages rather than trusting this table's status
column**. Report only what is OPEN or opening within ~60 days.

## Aggregate hubs — checked every weekly scan (catches new programs not yet in this table)

- **IAF program board:** bciaf.ca/programs/ — single source of truth for EFP/BMP/ERP/CAF status. Any new intake or program listed here goes on the core-stack watchlist.
- **BCWGC funding hub:** bcwgc.org/funding-opportunities/ — the council curates exactly our niche. Newly listed provincial or federal programs of relevance show up here first.
- **BC Grape Growers' Association funding page:** grapegrowers.bc.ca/funding — weekly pass.
- **gov.bc.ca agriculture programs:** www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/agriculture-seafood/programs — weekly pass.

If the aggregate page lists a program not in the core stack above, add it to the watchlist with its URL and what it funds, and report it once with the new addition.

## Water & drought notices — same scan, second half

Six vineyards in a semi-arid valley live or die by irrigation rules. On the SAME weekly pass,
check the **BC Drought Information Portal** (government of BC drought levels — the Okanagan
basin level drives voluntary/mandatory cuts) and the **Okanagan Basin Water Board /
local water purveyor** pages covering Penticton, Oliver and Osoyoos rural areas for restriction
notices. Report: current basin drought level (with change since last scan), any stage
restrictions affecting agricultural use, and days-until-effective where stated. In a level-4/5
summer this line is as important as anything else in the daily report.

## Judgement rules when reporting

1. **Lead with actionability**: open-now + relevant beats bigger-but-closed. ERP "coming fall"
   is worth a line BECAUSE freeze-damaged southern blocks make us plausible applicants — say so.
2. **Always state the prerequisite**: BMP needs an EFP number first; if we don't hold one,
   that is the actual recommendation ("start the free EFP now so BMP eligibility exists").
3. **Deadlines get dates**, never "soon". Convert to days-remaining.
4. Never invent amounts — quote ranges only as shown on the program page you just read.
5. One-line-per-program in the daily report unless something NEW opened; full detail goes in the
   weekly digest. Grants are not weather — do not manufacture urgency that isn't there.
